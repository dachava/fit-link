  - Elbows inside the knees at the bottom
  - Drive the floor apart with the feet

  - Heels lifting at depth
  - Dumbbell drifting away from the sternum
  - Knees caving inward on the way up
---

## Setup
Hold one dumbbell vertically against the chest, cupping the top end with both hands.
Feet just outside shoulder width, toes turned slightly out.

## Execution
Sit down between the hips, keeping the dumbbell close to the chest and the torso
upright. Descend until the elbows brush the inside of the knees, then drive
through the whole foot to stand back up.
