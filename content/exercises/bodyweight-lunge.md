primary_muscle: quadriceps
secondary_muscles: [glutes, hamstrings]
equipment: [bodyweight]
category: compound
cues:
  - Step far enough forward for both knees near 90 degrees
  - Keep the torso upright, don't lean forward
  - Push through the front heel to return to standing
common_faults:
  - Front knee traveling far past the toes
  - Back knee slamming into the floor
  - Torso leaning forward instead of staying upright
---

## Setup
Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, hands on hips or at the sides.

## Execution
Step forward with one leg and lower the back knee toward the floor until
both knees are near 90 degrees. Push through the front heel to return to
the starting position, then repeat on the other side.
