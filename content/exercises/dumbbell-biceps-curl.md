  - Elbows pinned to the sides through the whole rep

  - Swinging the torso to generate momentum
  - Elbows drifting forward
  - Only doing the top half of the range of motion

## Setup
Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand, arms fully extended, elbows close to
the torso.

## Execution
Curl both dumbbells up toward the shoulders, keeping the elbows fixed in
place. Squeeze briefly at the top, then lower under control to full
extension.
