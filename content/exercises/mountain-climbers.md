name: Mountain Climbers
primary_muscle: core
secondary_muscles: [shoulders, hip flexors]
equipment: [bodyweight]
category: conditioning
cues:
  - Keep the hips low and level, like a moving plank
  - Drive the knees toward the chest, not out to the sides
  - Hands stay planted under the shoulders throughout
common_faults:
  - Hips popping up into a piked position
  - Losing the flat back as fatigue sets in
  - Feet barely leaving the floor instead of driving the knee forward
---

## Setup
Start in a push-up position, hands under the shoulders, body in a straight
line from head to heels.

## Execution
Drive one knee toward the chest, then quickly switch legs, keeping the hips
low and the pace controlled but continuous.
