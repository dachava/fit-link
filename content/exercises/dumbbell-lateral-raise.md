cues:
  - Lead with the elbows, not the hands
  - Raise to about shoulder height, no higher
  - Slight forward lean of the torso, don't lean back
common_faults:
  - Using heavy momentum/swinging to raise the weight
  - Shrugging the shoulders up toward the ears
  - Raising past shoulder height
---

## Setup
Stand holding a light dumbbell in each hand at the sides, palms facing in,
slight bend in the elbows.

## Execution
Raise both arms out to the sides until roughly parallel with the floor,
leading with the elbows. Lower with control back to the sides.
