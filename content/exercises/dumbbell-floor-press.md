cues:
  - Tuck elbows to about 45 degrees, not flared to 90
  - Pause briefly when the triceps touch the floor
  - Drive the dumbbells straight up, not back toward the face
common_faults:
  - Bouncing the elbows off the floor instead of pausing
  - Flaring elbows out to the sides
  - Arching the lower back off the floor
---

## Setup
Lie on the floor (or a mat) with knees bent, feet flat. Hold a dumbbell in each
hand at chest level, palms facing the feet.

## Execution
Press both dumbbells straight up until the arms are extended, then lower with
control until the backs of the upper arms touch the floor. Pause, then press
back up.
