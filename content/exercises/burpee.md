  - Skipping the chest-to-floor push-up

  - Rounding the back when hinging down to the floor

## Setup
Stand tall with feet shoulder-width apart.

## Execution
Squat down and place the hands on the floor, jump the feet back to a plank,
perform a push-up, jump the feet back up to the hands, then jump straight up
with arms overhead.
