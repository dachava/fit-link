  - Hinge at the hips, flat back, soft knees
  - Pull the elbows back, not up toward the ears

  - Rounding the lower back
  - Using momentum/torso swing instead of the back
  - Standing too upright, turning it into a shrug

## Setup
Hold a dumbbell in each hand. Hinge forward from the hips until the torso is
roughly 45 degrees to the floor, knees slightly bent, back flat.

## Execution
Row both dumbbells toward the lower ribs, driving the elbows back and
squeezing the shoulder blades together. Lower under control to a full arm
extension.
