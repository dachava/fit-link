name: Push-Up
primary_muscle: chest
secondary_muscles: [triceps, shoulders, core]
equipment: [bodyweight]
category: compound
cues:
  - Straight line from head to heels the whole rep
  - Elbows at roughly 45 degrees, not flared to 90
  - Full lockout at the top
common_faults:
  - Hips sagging toward the floor
  - Flaring elbows straight out to the sides
  - Cutting the range of motion short at the bottom
---

## Setup
Hands on the floor slightly wider than shoulder width, body in a straight
line from head to heels, core braced.

## Execution
Lower the chest toward the floor by bending the elbows, keeping the body
rigid. Press back up to full arm extension.
