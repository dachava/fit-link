equipment: [bodyweight]
category: isolation
cues:
  - Squeeze glutes and brace the core like expecting a punch
  - Straight line from head to heels
  - Push the floor away through the forearms
common_faults:
  - Hips sagging toward the floor
  - Hips piked too high in the air
  - Holding the breath instead of breathing steadily
---

## Setup
Forearms on the floor, elbows under the shoulders, body in a straight line
from head to heels, toes tucked under.

## Execution
Hold the straight-line position, bracing the core and squeezing the glutes,
for the target duration without letting the hips sag or pike.
