cues:
  - Full effort on each sprint, not a jog
  - Walk back to the start as the recovery, don't rush it
  - Drive the arms to help drive the legs
common_faults:
  - Starting too fast on the first rep and fading badly by the last
  - Cutting the recovery walk short and pacing every rep the same
  - Sprinting on an uneven or unsafe surface
---

## Setup
Find a flat, clear backyard stretch of at least 30-40 meters with a marked
start and finish point.

## Execution
Sprint at full effort from the start point to the finish point, then walk
back to the start as recovery before the next rep.
