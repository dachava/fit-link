equipment: [dumbbell]
category: compound
cues:
  - Brace the core, ribs stacked over the hips
  - Press up and slightly back, finishing over the ear
  - Full lockout at the top without shrugging
common_faults:
  - Arching the lower back to gain range of motion
  - Pressing the dumbbells forward instead of overhead
  - Flaring elbows too wide on the way up
---

## Setup
Stand or sit holding a dumbbell in each hand at shoulder height, palms facing
forward, core braced.

## Execution
Press both dumbbells straight overhead until the arms are fully extended,
then lower with control back to shoulder height.
