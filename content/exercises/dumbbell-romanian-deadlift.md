cues:
  - Push the hips back first, knees stay soft
  - Keep the dumbbells close, brushing the thighs
  - Stop the descent when the hamstrings feel fully loaded
common_faults:
  - Turning it into a squat by bending the knees too much
  - Rounding the back to reach lower
  - Letting the dumbbells drift forward away from the legs
---

## Setup
Stand holding a dumbbell in each hand in front of the thighs, feet hip-width
apart.

## Execution
Push the hips back while lowering the dumbbells down the front of the legs,
keeping a flat back and soft knees. Lower until a strong hamstring stretch is
felt, then drive the hips forward to return to standing.
