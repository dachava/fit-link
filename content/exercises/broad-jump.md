cues:
  - Swing the arms back then forward to drive the jump
  - Land soft with bent knees, absorb the landing
  - Stick the landing before resetting for the next rep
common_faults:
  - Landing stiff-legged
  - Not resetting fully between reps, rushing the next jump
  - Leaning too far forward and landing off balance
---

## Setup
Stand with feet hip-width apart in an open backyard space with clear room
ahead to land.

## Execution
Swing the arms back and bend the knees, then explode forward and up,
swinging the arms forward. Land softly with bent knees, absorbing the
impact, and stick the landing before resetting.
